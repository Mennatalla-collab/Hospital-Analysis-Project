4. Hospital Performance & Overview Dashboard

Project Overview
An operational Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate healthcare performance metrics, track patient admission trends, analyze staff distribution and morale, and monitor service capacity and refusal rates across hospital departments.

Key Insights
* Patient Care & Operational Volume: Out of 13,493 total patient requests, 5,851 patients were admitted across 126 staff members, achieving an overall attendance rate of 89.58% and maintaining a 7-day average length of stay.
* Departmental Satisfaction & Morale: ICU scored the highest in patient satisfaction (81.62), while Emergency registered the highest staff morale (73.56) despite handling the highest volume of patient refusals (5.0K).
* Staffing & Service Capacity: Nurses represent the largest workforce segment (73 staff members), with admission volume peaking at 168 admissions in week 50.

Tools Used
Power BI | Data Modeling | DAX | Page Navigation Buttons | Interactive Slicers
